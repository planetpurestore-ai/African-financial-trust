How AFT should be used

Responsible Use and Limitations

AFT’s aim is to power better-informed institutional lending decisions by making business evidence easier to review. AFT supports that process; it does not make the decision.

Evidence-status categories

Self-declared

Information supplied by a business or user without an independent check.

Document-extracted

Information extracted from a submitted document. Extraction is not proof that the document or fact is genuine.

Counterparty-confirmed

Information confirmed by an identified counterparty through an authorised workflow.

Independently verified

Information checked against an independent source or approved process. This label must only be used where the check actually occurred.

Required safeguards

Use human governance and appropriate due diligence before an adverse lending or commercial decision. Do not use AFT to unlawfully discriminate, infer sensitive characteristics, evade consumer-protection duties, or present an evidence label as a guarantee of creditworthiness or repayment. Demonstrations use synthetic data unless real-data processing is expressly authorised and legally supported.

Regulatory boundary

AFT does not issue regulated credit ratings, operate as a lender, or replace licensed financial institutions, credit bureaux, payment providers, tax authorities, registries, or regulators. Any future expansion will depend on the relevant legal and regulatory pathway.
