Terms of Use

These terms are a working public summary for a controlled demonstration and pilot. They are not a substitute for a signed customer agreement and should be reviewed by counsel before production use.

What AFT provides

AFT provides software for collecting, organising, assessing, recording, and presenting business evidence. The intended use is to support better-informed institutional lending review and commercial decisions.

What AFT does not provide

AFT is not a bank, lender, payment provider, credit bureau, credit-rating provider, or licensed financial institution. AFT does not approve or decline loans, make regulated credit decisions, guarantee repayment, or replace a lender’s due diligence, governance, or human judgement.

Evidence and customer responsibilities

Customers must have the right to submit information and must ensure that it is accurate, lawful, and suitable for the agreed purpose. Evidence labels describe how information entered the platform was obtained; they do not make every underlying fact true. Customers remain responsible for decisions made using the platform.

Pilots, availability and data

Access may be limited, changed, suspended, or withdrawn while AFT is in pilot. No live external integration is promised unless it is separately documented and tested. Data-processing, confidentiality, security, service levels, intellectual property, liability, and dispute terms will be set out in the applicable signed agreement.
