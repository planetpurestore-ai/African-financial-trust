Privacy Notice

This public notice is a plain-English summary for the current controlled demonstration and pilot stage. It should be reviewed and completed by Rwanda counsel before production onboarding.

African Financial Trust (AFT) is developing B2B financial-trust and verification infrastructure. Depending on the service and the instructions received, AFT may act as a data controller, processor, or both. The responsible legal entity and contact details will be updated after incorporation.

Information we may handle

Information may include business identity and registration details, contact details, invoices, purchase orders, contracts, accounting records, transaction and payment evidence, counterparty information, access logs, and communications. Demonstrations should use synthetic data unless real-data processing is explicitly authorised and legally supported.

Why we use it

We use information to operate and secure the platform, organise and assess evidence, produce evidence-status records, support authorised institutional lending review and commercial decisions, respond to enquiries, improve the service, and meet legal or security obligations. We do not sell personal data.

Lawful basis, retention and sharing

Processing will be based on the applicable consent, contract, legitimate-interest, legal-obligation, or other lawful basis identified for the engagement. Retention will be limited to the agreed purpose and legal requirements. Information may be shared with authorised customer users, approved service providers, professional advisers, or authorities where legally required. Any cross-border transfer will be assessed and documented as required.

Your requests

Subject to applicable law and the customer’s role as controller, individuals may ask about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent. Send privacy questions to hello@africanfinancialtrust.com. AFT will update this notice as its legal entity, processing activities, and Rwanda data-protection arrangements are formalised.
