Trust is the missing infrastructure.

African businesses generate enormous amounts of commercial evidence every day. But that evidence often sits across invoices, contracts, purchase orders, bank accounts, mobile-money platforms, accounting systems and fragmented counterparties.

The result is a trust gap. Good businesses can struggle to prove the quality of their transactions, while lenders and financial institutions struggle to see enough reliable information to finance them efficiently.

African Financial Trust is building the infrastructure that closes that gap.

The Problem

African commerce produces evidence. The system doesn't connect it.

The information needed to understand a business transaction already exists. It is simply fragmented across too many systems, documents and relationships.

01 / EVIDENCE

Fragmented records

Invoices, contracts, purchase orders and financial records often live in separate places with no unified transaction view.

02 / VERIFICATION

Hard to verify

Institutions have limited infrastructure for independently checking whether transaction evidence is complete, consistent and trustworthy.

03 / INTELLIGENCE

Weak transaction intelligence

Important signals about counterparties, payment behaviour and commercial relationships remain buried inside raw records.

04 / FINANCE

Capital stays constrained

When trust is difficult to establish, otherwise financeable businesses and transactions can remain outside efficient financing systems.

How The Trust Engine Works

From fragmented evidence to trusted transaction intelligence.

The Trust Engine connects the evidence around a transaction, verifies it and transforms it into structured intelligence that financial institutions and businesses can use.

STEP 01

Collect

Connect invoices, purchase orders, contracts, accounting records, bank and mobile-money activity, counterparties and other relevant transaction evidence.

STEP 02

Verify

Cross-check the evidence to identify relationships, inconsistencies and signals that help establish whether the underlying transaction is genuine and coherent.

STEP 03

Understand

Turn raw evidence into transaction intelligence, revealing patterns around counterparties, payment behaviour, business activity and commercial relationships.

STEP 04

Finance

Produce a clearer trust profile that helps financial institutions and financing partners make better-informed decisions around financeable transactions.

THE FOUNDER

Built from a belief in Africa's economic potential.

Harry Mpore
Founder, African Financial Trust

Born in Rwanda, Harry Mpore founded African Financial Trust around a simple conviction: African businesses should not be held back by fragmented financial information or the difficulty of proving that legitimate transactions can be trusted.

African Financial Trust is his vision for building the infrastructure that helps make African commerce more verifiable, transparent and financeable — ultimately creating a trusted foundation for businesses and financial institutions across the continent.

His ambition is to help build a more trusted and connected African economy, where businesses can establish credibility, access finance and ultimately transact with greater confidence across borders.

Our Vision

Building the trust layer for African commerce.

African businesses generate enormous economic activity every day, yet the evidence behind many transactions remains fragmented across invoices, contracts, purchase orders, financial records, payment systems and counterparties.

African Financial Trust is being built to change that.

We begin by making business transactions verifiable — connecting fragmented evidence and turning it into trusted financial intelligence that businesses and financial institutions can rely on.

From that foundation, our vision is to expand into broader financial intelligence, financing infrastructure and cross-border commerce, creating a trusted layer that helps businesses establish credibility, access capital and transact with greater confidence across Africa.

Ultimately, we want to help create an African economy where trust can move with the transaction — regardless of the business, institution or border involved.

Our ambition

To become the trusted infrastructure layer behind African commerce.
