THE FOUNDER

Built from a belief in Africa's economic potential.

Harry Mpore
Founder, African Financial Trust

Born in Rwanda, Harry Mpore founded African Financial Trust around a simple conviction: African businesses should not be held back by fragmented financial information or the difficulty of proving that legitimate transactions can be trusted.

African Financial Trust is his vision for building the infrastructure that helps make African commerce more verifiable, transparent and financeable — ultimately creating a trusted foundation for businesses and financial institutions across the continent.

His ambition is to help build a more trusted and connected African economy, where businesses can establish credibility, access finance and ultimately transact with greater confidence across borders.

OUR VISION

Building the trust layer for African commerce.

Our Vision

African businesses generate enormous economic activity every day, yet the evidence behind many transactions remains fragmented across invoices, contracts, purchase orders, financial records, payment systems and counterparties.

African Financial Trust is being built to change that.

We begin by making business transactions verifiable — connecting fragmented evidence and turning it into trusted financial intelligence that businesses and financial institutions can rely on.

From that foundation, our vision is to expand into broader financial intelligence, financing infrastructure and cross-border commerce, creating a trusted layer that helps businesses establish credibility, access capital and transact with greater confidence across Africa.

Ultimately, we want to help create an African economy where trust can move with the transaction — regardless of the business, institution or border involved.

Our ambition

To become the trusted infrastructure layer behind African commerce.
