The infrastructure gap

Africa doesn't lack transactions. It lacks verifiable evidence.

African businesses generate invoices, purchase orders, contracts, payments and financial records every day.


But that evidence is often fragmented across different systems, institutions and counterparties.


African Financial Trust exists to connect that evidence and create a trusted infrastructure layer around the transaction.

The problem

Valuable transactions are difficult to verify.

Financial institutions need reliable evidence before they can confidently finance businesses and transactions.

01

Fragmented data

Critical transaction information exists across disconnected documents, systems and accounts.

02

Limited visibility

Financial institutions often cannot see the complete picture behind an SME transaction.

03

Verification friction

Manually checking counterparties and supporting evidence takes time and creates operational costs.

04

Missed finance

Good businesses and legitimate transactions can become harder to finance when trust is difficult to establish.
