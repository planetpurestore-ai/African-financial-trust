Partner on a controlled pilot

AFT is seeking conversations with banks, SACCOs, MFIs, lenders, businesses, and other institutions that want clearer evidence for lending review and commercial decisions.

No unrestricted production onboarding yet.
Initial work is controlled and scoped. Synthetic data should be used for demonstrations; real-data processing requires explicit authorisation, appropriate agreements, and a lawful basis.

What a pilot can explore

A pilot may map a lending or trade workflow, organise invoices and supporting records, assess evidence status, record provenance and counterparty confirmation, and measure whether reviewers receive clearer information. AFT will not approve loans or claim live registry, tax, bank, mobile-money, regulator, or other provider integrations unless a connection has been separately authorised, implemented, and tested.
